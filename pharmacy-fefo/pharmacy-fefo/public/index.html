Never dispense an expired batch again.

PharmaBatch is batch-aware pharmacy inventory software. It tracks every medicine down to the individual batch and expiry date, automatically dispenses from the soonest-expiring stock first (FEFO), and always shows you the true in-date count — not just a raw quantity that includes stock you can no longer sell.

Key features

📦 Batch tracking

Every stock entry is a batch: batch number, manufacture date, expiry date and quantity. One medicine, many batches, full traceability.

⏩ FEFO dispensing

Dispense a quantity and the system automatically deducts from the batch expiring soonest, spilling over into the next batch if needed — the way a real pharmacist should sell.

✅ Accurate in-date stock

Stock counts only include non-expired batches, so what you see on screen is what you can actually sell — expired stock is tracked separately, never silently counted.

🔍 Fast search

Find medicines instantly by name, generic name or category, with paginated, sortable results.

⚠️ Expiry & low-stock alerts

See what's expiring soon, what's already expired but still on the shelf, and what's below reorder level — before it becomes a problem.

🔐 Secure accounts

Simple registration and login so every dispense is tied to the staff member who made it.

Who it's for

Independent pharmacies, small hospital dispensaries and pharmacy chains that need to stay compliant with expiry regulations, cut down wastage from expired stock, and give staff a fast, reliable way to check what's actually available to dispense right now.
